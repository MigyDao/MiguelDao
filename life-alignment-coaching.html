A collaborative approach

Curiosity, structure and your autonomy.

We might discuss your current circumstances, personal values, competing demands, aspirations, decisions or the systems and relationships that support you. The pace and emphasis adapt to what you bring. You are never required to share intimate details, adopt my beliefs or commit to a particular course of action.

Life coaching versus practical working support

Life Alignment Coaching focuses on reflection, personal direction and choices. If you mainly want someone to help organize, begin or complete a specific task together, the existing Personal Reset & Work Session may be a better fit.

Clear boundaries

This is peer-to-peer, nonclinical guidance, not psychotherapy, diagnosis, medical or crisis support, or licensed legal or financial advice. I do not claim a clinical or professional coaching credential. No particular life outcome is guaranteed. Please share only the context needed to discuss your goals; we will clarify privacy and suitability before payment. If your needs require specialist care, I will suggest finding an appropriately qualified provider.

Refunds and session credits

Every completed session has a 100% satisfaction guarantee: if it does not feel meaningfully useful, reply to the follow-up or email me for a full refund of that session's original paid unit fee. The request window runs through seven calendar days after you receive the written recap; you can also request a refund before the recap arrives. No explanation or survey is required.

Unused Alignment Journey credits are refundable at their original package unit value of J$7,000 / US$45 each, with no discount clawback, processing-fee deduction or forced store credit. Completed refunded sessions are not also unused credits. Rescheduling and cancellation arrangements are confirmed reasonably before payment; if I cannot deliver, you can choose an appropriate reschedule or refund. These terms do not restrict applicable consumer rights.
