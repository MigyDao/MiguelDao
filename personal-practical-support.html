Practical support, clear boundaries

This is collaborative, nonclinical peer support—not psychotherapy, diagnosis, medical or crisis support, regulated legal or financial advice, or outsourced administration. You remain responsible for your decisions and work. No diagnosis is required, and no particular outcome is guaranteed. Share only the practical context needed; please avoid sensitive details.

7-calendar-day 100% satisfaction guarantee—every completed session

If a completed session does not feel meaningfully useful, reply to your session follow-up or email Miguel for a refund of 100% of the fee paid for that session. Each session has its own seven-calendar-day guarantee period starting when you receive its written recap. You may also request a refund before the recap arrives. No explanation or survey is required.

For a prepaid package, each completed session is covered at its original discounted per-session price. You may also request a refund for any unused prepaid credits at that same original unit price: Momentum JMD 6,000 / USD 38 each; Build & Practice JMD 5,700 / USD 36 each. There is no discount clawback, penalty or mandatory store credit. Seller processing fees do not reduce your promised refund. These terms do not restrict applicable consumer rights.

Rescheduling, cancellations and no-shows are handled reasonably, case by case, with the arrangements confirmed before payment. If Miguel cannot deliver or a scope mismatch is identified before delivery, you can choose an appropriate reschedule or refund.

How to begin

  1. In the enquiry form below, confirm One-to-one personal management support (select it if needed), then choose your preferred session option.
  2. Miguel reviews suitability first and clarifies any missing context, recap timing and cancellation arrangements before requesting payment.
  3. Jamaican clients receive private instructions for an existing bank-transfer or Lynk arrangement. International clients receive a PayPal request or invoice in USD. No payment is taken by this form.
  4. After payment or an eligible prepaid credit is confirmed, receive a fresh single-use link for one 90-minute Google Meet session. Repeat that step separately for each package session.
