IWTM Custom Fabrication Operations

Building a repeatable operating flow around custom fabrication, from customer enquiry through procurement, production and delivery.

What this demonstrates

  • Operations
  • Workflow design
  • Quoting
  • Procurement
  • Production coordination
  • Customer communication

The problem

Custom work creates an information problem as much as a making problem.

Each custom fabrication job can bring a different brief, dimensions, material requirement, quote, supplier order, production sequence and delivery condition. Without a repeatable operating flow, key information ends up distributed across messages, memory, spreadsheets and workshop activity.

My role

Working across both the operating layer and the physical-delivery layer.

  • Customer communication and requirement capture.
  • Quotation preparation.
  • Sourcing and purchasing.
  • Build-plan and production preparation.
  • Production coordination and supplier follow-up.
  • Delivery coordination and practical job tracking.

Operating pattern

Enquiry → clarify → quote → source → build → quality check → deliver.

The useful capability here is integration. Instead of treating every job as an isolated build, the work developed into a more repeatable path from customer request through purchasing, production and delivery.

That pattern now informs how I approach project systems, costing, client delivery and maker-business operations.

Evidence boundary

Strong workflow evidence without forcing disputed historical metrics.

Older portfolio and résumé versions contain conflicting build counts, revenue totals, delivery rates and margin figures. Those numbers are intentionally omitted here until they can be reconciled against dated records.

The case stands on the documented role and workflow itself rather than depending on an inflated headline statistic.
