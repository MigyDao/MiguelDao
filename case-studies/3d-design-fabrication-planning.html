How I approach planning

Make the important decisions visible early.

  • Understand the space: gather available measurements, reference images, practical constraints and the intended use.
  • Explore the layout: visualize how furniture, cabinets or other components fit and relate to one another.
  • Review proportions and access: use model views to discuss clearances, usability and possible adjustments.
  • Plan the next step: where included in the scope, develop component information, material estimates or fabrication guidance from a reviewed design.
