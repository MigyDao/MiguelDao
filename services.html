Good to know

Common questions, short answers.

Exact details are confirmed before any booking or project agreement.

Can I drop off an item for repair or modification?

Yes, by prior arrangement. Drop-off repairs start at J$1,000 in labour, with the final price depending on the size and scope of the repair or modification, the item's condition, materials and time. The lowest-cost option is to bring the item and collect it; some jobs may be completed while you wait if practical, while others need more time for adhesive curing or other steps. Collection, delivery and other logistics are separately quoted.

How does an on-site day work?

Individual hands-on work starts at J$12,000 for a minimum one-day booking, with up to eight hours at the site including an agreed meal break. Start time, priorities and site access are confirmed in advance. Extra days or scope changes need approval.

Are tools, travel and materials included?

Not automatically. A suitable compact tool kit may be brought after confirming the job. Travel and mobilization, project materials, consumables, specialist equipment and helpers are supplied by the client or included in a separate agreed quote.

What are the payment and cancellation terms?

For first-time on-site clients, labour is payable at the end of each booked day; agreed travel costs are funded or arranged before departure. Multi-day projects may use deposits or milestones. Rescheduling is generally free at least 48 hours ahead, except agreed nonrecoverable costs. Late cancellations may incur charges if specified and agreed before booking, with reasonable emergency and safety exceptions.

Are all nine services available anywhere?

The categories are available within the defined scope and resources. Work requiring a site visit depends on location, travel and equipment. Ecological gardening is primarily assessment and planning for now, not grass cutting or powered grounds maintenance. Plumbing, fixed mains electrical work, regulated spraying and specialist unsafe work are excluded.

Can I request something not listed?

Yes. Describe the goal and constraints through the project form. I'll explain whether it fits my capabilities or needs a separately qualified collaborator.
